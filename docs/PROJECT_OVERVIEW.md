# Project Overview — SchengeN Routes

## Vision & Purpose

**SchengeN Routes** (_"Your Gateway to Europe — Study • Travel • Explore"_) is a modular European education, visa-information, travel-planning, and cultural discovery platform designed to provide structured, trustworthy guidance and convert visitors into genuine consultation enquiries.

## Core Service Architecture

1. **Study (`/study`) **: Country overviews, university profiles, Bachelor's/Master's/Foundation programmes, scholarships, admission requirements, and verified application intakes.
2. **Visa Guide (`/visa-guide`)**: Schengen short-stay and national student visa guides, document checklists, application steps, FAQs, official sources, and last-reviewed dates. (Educational guidance only; consular decisions rest solely with official authorities.)
3. **Travel (`/travel`)**: Destination guides, suggested itineraries, transport and accommodation guidance, and verified travel assistance services.
4. **Explore (`/explore`)**: Editorial magazine covering European cities, culture, landmarks, student life, and local experiences.

## Core Principles

- **Accuracy & Verifiability**: Never invent deadlines, tuition fees, partnerships, or team credentials. Always cite official sources.
- **Modularity**: Independent component and route boundaries per service pillar under a unified brand system.
- **Privacy & Security**: GDPR-aware enquiry handling, strict server-side secret isolation, and input validation.
