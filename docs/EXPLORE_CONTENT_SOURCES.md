---

## 2. Verification Rules & Anti-Fabrication Standards

- SchengeN Routes presents concise original editorial summaries and links to external authorities rather than copying external passages or claiming original historical authorship.
- Unverified historical claims, UNESCO listing dates, opening hours, admission fees, festival dates, experience prices, visitor counts, fake author personas, and fabricated reviews are strictly omitted.
- Every record carries an explicit `lastReviewed` or `updatedAt` ISO date (`2026-11-24`).
