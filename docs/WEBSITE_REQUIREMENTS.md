# Website Requirements — SchengeN Routes

## Navigation & Pages

- **Primary Navigation:** Home | Study | Visa Guide | Travel | Explore | About Us | Contact + prominent **Get Consultation** CTA button.
- **Dedicated Service Sections:** Independent landing pages and dynamic internal routes for Study, Visa Guide, Travel, and Explore.
- **Legal & Compliance Pages:** Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, Cookie Policy.

## Functional Requirements

- **Dynamic Consultation Enquiry Form:** Adapts fields based on selected service (`Study`, `Visa Guide`, `Travel`, `Explore`) with client + server validation and spam protection.
- **WhatsApp Integration:** Direct business WhatsApp contact (`+36 70 792 3072`) separate from the official WhatsApp Community link.
- **Structured Data Models:** Reusable TypeScript records for countries, universities, programmes, scholarships, visa guides, destinations, and articles.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **Responsive Design:** Full support from `320px` small mobile screens up to `1920px+` desktop displays with zero horizontal scrolling.
- **Accessibility & SEO:** Semantic HTML, WCAG AA element contrast, keyboard navigation, per-page metadata, sitemap, and robots configuration.
